# Robert Park

## Who he is

In The Impossible Press, Robert Park is one of the social theorists Rosen turns to in analyzing the new kind of social body the mass circulation newspaper created. The dissertation does not give a separate account of Park's career; it cites him as part of the effort to describe a public that is neither a traditional community nor merely a crowd.

## In the dissertation

Rosen invokes Park in Chapter 4 (p. 117), where the mass circulation newspaper produces a social body with ambiguous properties: as a way of tying people together the newspaper has a dissolving as well as a connecting tendency, weakening local ties while creating abstract, unstable bonds. Park reappears in Chapter 5 (p. 152), in the account of a public gathered together without actually meeting, where the system of communication is addressed not at the public but at private individuals hooked into long lines of communication from remote sources.
