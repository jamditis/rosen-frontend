# James Carey

## Who he is

James Carey was a professor and journalism dean at Columbia University. In The Impossible Press, Rosen draws on Carey's way of thinking about communication to frame the press and the public not as two fixed things but as parts of a larger relationship that has to be understood together.

## In the dissertation

Carey appears in the conclusion (p. 374), where Rosen lays out an ecological understanding of the press-public relationship. Rosen identifies five factors that must be inserted between press and public for an adequate account: the size and scale of the world to be informed about, the character of the social bond among audience members, the structural characteristics of mass media, the nature of language and symbols, and the professional attitude. His most important finding there is that the professional attitude is a very limited view of press and public.
