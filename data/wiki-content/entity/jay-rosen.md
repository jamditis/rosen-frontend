# Jay Rosen

## Who he is

Jay Rosen is a professor of journalism at New York University, where he has taught since 1986. He is the author of the PressThink blog and one of the most widely read critics of the American press. His work returns again and again to a single question: what is the press for, and what would it mean for journalism to actually inform the public?

## The dissertation at the root

In 1986 Rosen completed his doctoral dissertation, The Impossible Press: American Journalism and the Decline of Public Life, written at NYU under Neil Postman. Its argument, that journalism is a transaction between the press and the public rather than a one-way delivery of facts, runs through four decades of his later criticism. The 21 key ideas in this wiki are drawn from that dissertation.
