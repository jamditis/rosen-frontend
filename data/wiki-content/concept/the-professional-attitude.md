# The professional attitude obscures fundamental problems

## The idea

Professional journalism answers its critics with accuracy and fairness: get the facts right, give both sides. Rosen argues that this professional attitude, useful as it is, obscures the deeper structural factors that decide whether the press can inform the public at all. Reform aimed only at the conduct of individual journalists leaves those structures untouched.

## From The Impossible Press

An impossible press was born, one which sought to solve the whole problem of public life simply by controlling the conduct of journalists. (Chapter 6, p. 220)
