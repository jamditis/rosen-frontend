# More communication creates more to communicate about

## The idea

Better communication does not make informing the public easier. Rosen argues that every improvement expands the volume of events, issues, and voices competing for attention, so the task grows harder rather than simpler. The paradox is that the more we can communicate, the more there is to communicate about.

## From The Impossible Press

Improvements in communication also make communication more difficult because they ensure that there will be more to communicate about. (Chapter 3, p. 95)
