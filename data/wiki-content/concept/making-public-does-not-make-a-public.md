# Making things public does not make a public

## The idea

Putting information into the world is not the same as creating a public that can use it. Rosen draws a sharp line between publicity and a public: releasing facts accomplishes nothing if the conditions for understanding and action are absent. Making things public, by itself, does not make a public.

## From The Impossible Press

For any press anywhere, making things public does not a public make. (Conclusion, p. 400)
