# We respond to pictures in our heads, not reality

## The idea

We act not on the world as it is but on the pictures of it we carry in our heads, built from stereotypes, media images, and prior beliefs. Drawing on Walter Lippmann, Rosen argues that we define first and then see, so the press shapes reality by shaping the representations through which people perceive it.

## From The Impossible Press

The stereotype is a form of perception that works from private belief toward reality: we define first and then see. (Chapter 7, p. 282)
