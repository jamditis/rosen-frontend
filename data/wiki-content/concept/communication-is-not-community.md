# Communication does not equal community

## The idea

Connecting people through media is not the same as building a community among them. Rosen warns against the assumption that more communication automatically produces shared public life. Channels that link strangers can leave them more isolated, not more bound together, because genuine community depends on shared experience and obligation that broadcasting alone does not supply.

## Why it endures

The promise that a new network will knit society back together has followed the telegraph, radio, television, and the internet in turn. Rosen's distinction between communication and community explains why each wave of connection can coincide with a sense of fragmentation rather than cure it.
