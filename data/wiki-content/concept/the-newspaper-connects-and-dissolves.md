# The newspaper both connects and dissolves

## The idea

The mass newspaper ties millions of strangers into a shared daily experience, yet the same reach can loosen the local, face-to-face ties that sustain real community. Rosen describes this double motion: the press connects people in the abstract while dissolving the concrete bonds that public life depends on.

## From The Impossible Press

As a way of tying people together the newspaper has a dissolving as well as a connecting tendency. (Chapter 4, p. 140)
