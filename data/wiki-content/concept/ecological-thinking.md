# The press-public relationship requires ecological thinking

## The idea

Rosen ends by arguing that the press and the public can only be understood ecologically, as parts of one system. Five interacting factors shape that system: scale, social bonds, media structure, language and symbols, and professional attitudes. Because a change in any one ripples through the others, no single reform of journalism can fix the relationship on its own.

## From The Impossible Press

The trouble lies deeper than the press, and so does the remedy. (Chapter 7, p. 320)
