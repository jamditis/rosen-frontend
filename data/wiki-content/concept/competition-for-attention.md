# The competition for attention transforms public life

## The idea

When there is more information than anyone can absorb, attention becomes the scarce resource and the contest to capture it takes over. Rosen argues that once attention must be won rather than assumed, the struggle to be heard starts to crowd out the substance of public discourse. The form of the competition shapes what gets said.

## From The Impossible Press

When there is an indefinite supply of available information, attention is scarce. The battle for attention is then the very substance of communication. (Chapter 5, p. 180)
