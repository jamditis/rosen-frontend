# Information is relational

## The idea

Information, in Rosen's account, does not sit inside news items waiting to be delivered. It exists in the relationship between people and their changing environment, in what a fact means to someone trying to act on the world. The same report can inform one reader and mean nothing to another, because information is relational, not a substance the press hands over.

## From The Impossible Press

Information is not really "in" the items which come over the wire and make their way into the newspaper. It is "in" the relations between people and a changing environment. (Chapter 2, p. 58)
