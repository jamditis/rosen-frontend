# Communication is an art, not merely transmission

## The idea

Communicating with a public is an art, not a transmission. Rosen, following Dewey, argues that effective communication depends on understanding audiences and contexts rather than simply broadcasting facts. Like seeds, ideas have to be planted where they can take root, not scattered at random in the hope that some will land.

## From The Impossible Press

Seeds are sown not by being thrown out at random, but by being so distributed as to take root and have a chance of growth. (Chapter 8, p. 345)
