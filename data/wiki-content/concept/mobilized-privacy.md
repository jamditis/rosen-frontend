# Mobilized privacy replaces public life

## The idea

Modern media speaks to private individuals, not to an assembled public. Rosen calls the result mobilized privacy: each person hooked into long lines of communication from remote sources while consuming the same messages alone. We are connected to distant events and isolated from one another at the same time.

## From The Impossible Press

Our system of communication is not addressed at the public but at private individuals. We have evolved a radical form of mobilized privacy: the individual hooked into long lines of communication from remote sources. (Chapter 5, p. 160)
