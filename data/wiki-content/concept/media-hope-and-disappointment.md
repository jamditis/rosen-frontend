# Each new medium inspires and then disappoints

## The idea

Each new medium arrives wrapped in the same hope, that it will finally restore the small-scale, participatory democracy that scale destroyed. Rosen traces a recurring arc from utopian expectation to disappointment, as people discover that a communication technology cannot, by itself, solve problems that are social and structural in origin.

## Why it endures

Written before the web, the pattern has repeated with blogs, social media, and each new platform in turn: a burst of democratic promise, then the recognition that the underlying problems of public life remain. Rosen's arc is a standing caution against expecting the next tool to do the work that communities must do.
