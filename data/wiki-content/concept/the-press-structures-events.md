# The press structures events to its own demands

## The idea

The press does not hold up a mirror to the world. Rosen argues that media reshape events to fit their own requirements, drama, conflict, brevity, consumability, so that form shapes content and the medium biases the message. What reaches the public is already structured by the demands of the channel that carried it.

## Why it endures

The point anticipates decades of work on media framing and the logic of the feed, where what is measurable and shareable steers what is covered. Rosen's claim that the press structures events to its own demands explains why changing the medium changes the news, even when the facts stay the same.
