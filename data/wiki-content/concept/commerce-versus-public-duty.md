# The conflict between commerce and public duty is structural

## The idea

Journalism is a business that claims a public mission, and Rosen argues the conflict between the two is structural, not incidental. Yellow journalism exposed the contradiction in the open; professional standards were an attempt to manage it. But the tension between private profit and public duty cannot be dissolved by professionalism alone, because it is built into the enterprise.

## From The Impossible Press

Yellow journalism put a sharper edge on the conflict between private profits and public duties. (Chapter 6, p. 240)
