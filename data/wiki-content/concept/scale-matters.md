# Scale matters

## The idea

The ideals of democratic self-government were worked out for small, face-to-face communities. Stretching them across a continental republic of strangers creates problems of scale that the press is then asked to solve on its own. Rosen argues those problems are structural: they come from the size and complexity of modern society, not from any failure of reporters to try harder.

## From The Impossible Press

Till the Great Society is converted into a Great Community, the Public will remain in eclipse. (Chapter 8, p. 340)
