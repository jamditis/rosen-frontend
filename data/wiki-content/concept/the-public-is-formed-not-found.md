# The public is not found but formed

## The idea

There is no ready-made public sitting in wait for the morning's news. A public has to be formed, assembled around a shared understanding of common problems, and that takes more than distributing facts. Rosen argues that treating the public as something already there, only needing information, mistakes the hardest part of the press's job for a solved problem.

## From The Impossible Press

Providing information is not all there is to informing the public. (Conclusion, p. 380)
