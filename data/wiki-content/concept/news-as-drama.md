# News as drama turns citizens into spectators

## The idea

When news is told as drama, the reader's role quietly changes from participant to spectator. Rosen argues that the human interest story turns the journalist into a dramatist and public affairs into entertainment, so that citizens watch the show rather than act on what they learn.

## From The Impossible Press

The journalist becomes a dramatist; the audience becomes spectators rather than participants. (Chapter 2, p. 72)
