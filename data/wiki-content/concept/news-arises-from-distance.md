# News arises from distance

## The idea

Journalism exists because most of what matters happens beyond our direct experience. News is what crosses the distance between people and events they cannot witness firsthand. Rosen stresses that this distance is not only geographic but social and experiential, which is why the press can never simply show us the world as it is.

## From The Impossible Press

News arrives from a distance. It crosses the space between individuals and events. (Chapter 1, p. 13)
