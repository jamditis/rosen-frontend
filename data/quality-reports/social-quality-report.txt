FINDING: "Press & Media Criticism" appears in 95.6% of all categorized posts.
This is so dominant that it provides almost no discriminatory value as a filter.
Every post is effectively tagged with it, making it more of a global label than
a useful category.

FINDING: Nearly all posts have exactly 2 categories. This rigid structure
(always one primary + Press & Media Criticism) suggests the categorization was
done programmatically with a fixed rule: assign "Press & Media Criticism" plus
one other category. This reduces the value of multi-category filtering.

FINDING: "Journalism Education" is severely underrepresented at 1.5%. Given
that Jay Rosen is a professor who frequently discusses teaching, this suggests
the categorization pipeline may not adequately detect education-related content.

RECOMMENDATION:
  - Consider whether "Press & Media Criticism" should be treated as an implicit
    global tag rather than a filter category. Removing it from the category
    system would make the remaining 5 categories more meaningful for browsing.
  - Consider expanding the taxonomy with more specific categories (e.g.,
    "Election Coverage", "Trump/Media Dynamics", "Platform Criticism").
  - Investigate whether the 2-category-per-post pattern is a pipeline
    artifact or intentional.

PRIORITY 1 -- Data gaps requiring action before public release:

  (a) BACKFILL 2022 CATEGORIES: All 3,832 posts from 2022 have zero
      categories. This is a batch processing gap. Run the categorization
      pipeline on the 2021-2023 date range to fill this gap. Estimated
      impact: reduces uncategorized posts from 30.7% to approximately 17%.

  (b) FILTER NON-ROSEN BLUESKY POSTS: 54 posts by other authors will appear
      in the public archive. Add an author check to the export script:
        if (record.author !== 'Jay Rosen' && !record.id.startsWith('THREAD-'))
          return false;
      Or re-title them as "Quoted by [Author]" to let existing repost
      filtering handle them.

PRIORITY 2 -- Quality improvements:

  (c) KEYWORD-BASED CATEGORY INFERENCE: 5,078 uncategorized posts can have
      categories inferred from content keywords. This is a quick win using
      simple text matching before investing in AI classification.

  (d) BACKFILL KEY_CONCEPTS: At 1.5% coverage, this high-value field is
      nearly unused. A targeted pass using Rosen's known concepts as search
      terms could populate this for thousands of posts.

  (e) RECONSIDER "PRESS & MEDIA CRITICISM" DOMINANCE: At 95.6% coverage,
      this category provides almost no filtering value. Consider making it
      implicit and expanding the taxonomy.

PRIORITY 3 -- Structural improvements (non-blocking):

  (f) ADD is_repost FLAG: The current repost detection relies on title
      patterns. A structural boolean field would be more robust.

  (g) MOVE LOW-SIGNAL POSTS: The 2,114 emoji/affirmation/empty posts are
      correctly filtered from the public archive but still add processing
      overhead. Consider a separate table or CSV.

  (h) INVESTIGATE 2-CATEGORY PATTERN: The rigid 2-categories-per-post
      structure (99.8% of categorized posts) suggests a pipeline artifact.
      Verify this is intentional.

APPENDIX A: FILTERING PIPELINE SUMMARY
================================================================================

The export script (data/export-archive-data.js) applies these filters
sequentially:

  Step 1: Thread consolidation
    - Bluesky threads (3+ posts with responds_to chains) are consolidated
      into single THREAD records
    - Individual thread member posts are removed from the social feed
    - Existing THREAD records from archive_records-public.csv are preserved

  Step 2: Repost removal (REPOST_TITLE_PATTERN)
    - Pattern: /^(Quoted|Retweet|RT) by/i
    - Removes: 1,717 posts (all Twitter/X)
    - Result: Correct. All are non-Rosen content.

  Step 3: Short reply removal (GENERIC_TITLE_PATTERN + content check)
    - Pattern: /^(Reply|Tweet|Post|Quote) by/i
    - Then checks: content < 10 chars after stripping URLs, @mentions, emoji
    - Removes: 2,127 posts
    - Result: Correct. Catches all 571 "Tweet by" and 1,551 "Reply by" etc.

  Step 4: Final quality gate
    - Removes unverified records (social posts are auto-verified, so N/A)
    - Removes titles < 5 chars or "Untitled"
    - Removes records with no date AND no URL
    - Removes: 0 additional social posts

  Net result: ~25,288 social posts survive (before thread consolidation).

  GAP IN FILTERING: No author check. 54 non-Rosen Bluesky posts pass through.
