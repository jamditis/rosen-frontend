## LinkedIn

### Launch Post (Long-form)

**Headline:** Announcing the Jay Rosen Digital Archive: 36 Years of Journalism Criticism, Now Searchable

Today, we're launching a project I've been working on for [TIME]: the Jay Rosen Digital Archive.

**What it is:**
A comprehensive, searchable archive of journalism criticism by Jay Rosen, NYU professor and one of the most influential voices in media criticism for the past four decades.

**What's included:**
• 849+ records spanning 1986-2025
• The first public release of his 1986 doctoral dissertation
• Full-text search across all content
• 5,160+ extracted entities and 7,499+ mapped relationships
• Archival PDFs for every record

**Why it matters:**
Rosen's concepts—"the view from nowhere," "the people formerly known as the audience," "church of the savvy"—have shaped how we understand journalism's relationship to democracy. This archive preserves that intellectual contribution and makes it accessible for research, teaching, and public understanding.

For researchers, journalists, and anyone interested in how we got here with the press, the archive is free and open.
