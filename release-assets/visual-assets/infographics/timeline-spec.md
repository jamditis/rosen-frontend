## Key Milestones to Mark

### 1986
- **Dissertation completed** - "The Impossible Press: American Journalism and the Decline of Public Life"
- Started at NYU as journalism professor

### 1994
- **Public journalism movement** - Rosen becomes leading theorist/advocate

### 1999
- **"What Are Journalists For?" published** (Yale University Press)

### 2003
- **PressThink founded** - One of the earliest journalism criticism blogs

### 2006
- **NewAssignment.Net founded** - Experimental "open-source reporting" platform
- **"The People Formerly Known as the Audience"** - Defining post about audience transformation

### 2007
- **Assignment Zero** - Crowdsourced journalism experiment with Wired

### 2010
- **Studio 20 founded** at NYU - Graduate program for digital journalism innovation
- **"The View from Nowhere"** - Foundational critique of false objectivity

### 2011
- **"Why Political Coverage is Broken"** - Major analysis of horse-race journalism

### 2016-2020
- **Trump era coverage** - Extensive writing on press/presidency relationship
- **"Church of the Savvy"** concept gains prominence

### 2025
- **Jay Rosen Digital Archive launches**
