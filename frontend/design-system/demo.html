07 / restraint

Let the material set the volume

Expressive treatments belong to orientation and primary artifacts. Dense utility routes should let the system recede so records, relationships, and controls remain easy to scan.

Expressive when it orients

Use one strong field, signal panel, or archival frame to establish place and purpose.

Restrained when it repeats

Use paper, rules, compact rhythm, and predictable controls for results, entities, analytics, and research tools.

Specialized when it carries meaning

Keep the dissertation and Rosen 98 recognizable; share accessibility roles without flattening their identities.
