The logic of newspaper names is: what is our claim on you, the reader? This is what we will *be* for you. A guardian, a messenger, a register, a tribune. In our name is our promise. 20/

8:45 AM · May 22, 2019
Jay Rosen @jayrosen_nyu

The logic of newspaper names is a social logic. It projects a role in the community. (We're the ones who watch. We're the ones who keep the records. We're the ones who deliver the news fast.) 21/

8:45 AM · May 22, 2019
Jay Rosen @jayrosen_nyu

The logic of newspaper names is a journalistic logic. It describes what we *do*. (We announce, we review, we report, we inquire.) 22/

8:45 AM · May 22, 2019
Jay Rosen @jayrosen_nyu

In the 19th and early 20th century, all these roles were plausible and even inspiring. They could organize the work of a news company. Not just a brand, a name was *identity*. 23/
