The Jay Rosen Digital Archive

Welcome to a retro-inspired exploration of Jay Rosen's extensive body of work. This project serves as a digital record of his writings, spanning decades of critical thought on journalism, politics, and the role of the press in a democracy.

What is this?

This interface is designed to evoke the nostalgia of mid-90s operating systems while providing powerful ways to explore the archive:

  • Archive Explorer: Browse articles by thematic category, just like navigating folders.
  • Search Archive: Use the powerful search tool to find records by keywords, concepts, titles, and more.
  • Data Explorer: A visual tool to discover hidden relationships and connections between different records based on shared concepts.

This archive was created to preserve and provide new ways to interact with the important contributions of Jay Rosen to the field of journalism.
