User journey mapping to anticipate and plan for how different people will use the site:
Researcher path:
Enters via advanced search
Filters by date range and topic
Downloads citations (formatted references for academic papers)
Follows relationship links
Student path:
Starts with featured collections
Explores key concepts
Watches explanatory videos
Reads foundational texts
Journalist path:
Quick search for specific topic
Finds relevant quotes/concepts
Links to original sources
Shares specific items
