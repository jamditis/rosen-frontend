## Notes

**Most fruitful sources for further sweeping (in priority order):**
1. `pressthink.org/talks/` — Jay's own curated talks index, but it stops around 2011. Pre-2011 should be near-comprehensive from here.
2. `journalists.org/profiles/jay-rosen/` + per-year ONA speaker pages — every ONA appearance is catalogued.
3. `isoj.org/speakers-YYYY/jay-rosen/` — ISOJ tags him on every appearance (2014, 2019, 2023 confirmed; check 2015, 2016, 2017, 2018, 2020, 2021, 2022 as well).
4. `current.org/category/thepub/` — The Pub archive has at least #43 and #62 with Rosen; worth scanning the full archive.
5. `wnycstudios.org/podcasts/otm` — On the Media has many Rosen segments dating to ~2010; the WNYC people page (`https://www.wnyc.org/people/jay-rosen/`) is the canonical index but rendered too dynamically for WebFetch.
6. `rebootnews.wordpress.com` — full Rebooting The News episode list, 2009-2011, all featuring Rosen as co-host (NOT guest — flag separately).
7. `podchaser.com/creators/jay-rosen-107aNDpuES/appearances` — claims 8 podcast credits across 7 shows; behind a paywall for full episode list, but worth a manual scrape.
8. `niemanlab.org/tag/jay-rosen/` — Nieman Lab tag page lists every Rosen mention/appearance.
9. `journalismfestival.com/speaker/jay-rosen` — Perugia IJF speaker profile aggregates every year Rosen appeared (2018 confirmed; check 2014-2025).
10. The `youtube.com` channels for ONA, ISOJ, Berkman Klein, IJF Perugia, Knight Foundation, and Solutions Journalism Network — each likely has Rosen video the search engines don't surface.
