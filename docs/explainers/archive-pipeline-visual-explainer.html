Tagging and editorial meaning

Summary, pull quote, concepts, and notes

04

Summary

A short explanation of the Record's argument, context, or role in the Archive. This supports scan-friendly cards, modals, and editorial QA.

Sourceraw_text, title, date, publication, source notes.
ProcessingAI draft plus curator tightening for specificity and tone.
Frontend useRecord cards, search context, modal lead text, review scans.
Use: Tell Jay what this Record contributes in one tight paragraph, with enough specificity to distinguish it from nearby Records.

Pull quote

A representative excerpt that carries the Record's voice. It helps readers recognize the source and gives the modal a strong entry point.

Sourceverified source text, transcript, OCR, or recovered article body.
ProcessingExact excerpt selection, quote hygiene, length control.
Frontend useDetail modal, featured Records, editorial review, share copy.
Use: Preserve exact wording when quoting source text. Keep the quote attached to source provenance and review status.

Concept strings

Record-level idea labels: View from Nowhere, Citizens' Agenda, Mindcasting, Audience Atomization Overcome, The Production of Innocence, and related Rosen vocabulary.

SourceRecord argument, recurring Rosen vocabulary, dissertation-era themes.
ProcessingSchema-constrained concept assignment and normalization.
Frontend useModal context, filtering candidates, future concept pages.
Use: Keep these labels distinct from structured Concept Entities. The labels describe the Record; Entities power the graph.

Briefing close

Talk track for Jay

07
1
Start with the Record

The Record is the stable archival unit. Every feature starts from that row: evidence, metadata, classification, editorial fields, and relationships.

2
Separate fields by job

Identity fields cite the source. Classification fields power browsing. Tagging fields explain meaning. Entity fields create cross-Archive connections.

3
Use AI as processing support

AI drafts categories, summaries, concepts, and extractions. Schema validation, deduplication, and curator review keep the Archive coherent.

4
Show the frontend payoff

Static JSON gives the browser enough structure to support search, Facets, Record modals, the Entity Browser, and network exploration.

Each Record carries evidence, interpretation, and connections.

The Archive workflow turns source material into a durable public reference point, then links that reference point to the larger map of Rosen's people, institutions, ideas, works, and eras.
