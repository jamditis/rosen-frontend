The Impossible Press

American Journalism and the Decline of Public Life

Advisor: Neil Postman

In 1986, Jay Rosen completed his doctoral dissertation under Neil Postman at NYU. Nearly four decades later, his analysis of the troubled relationship between press and public reads less like historical scholarship and more like prophecy.

This interactive tool places his 1986 observations alongside the realities of 2025. What Rosen identified as emerging tendencies have become the dominant features of our media environment. The "impossible press" he described has only become more impossible.
